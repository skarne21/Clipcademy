# Moderator script and interview guide

Read the following instructions consistently.

“You have already encountered DNA replication in class. Spend up to ten minutes reviewing synthesis direction and primers using the assigned material. Then answer a short concept check. You may stop at any time. This does not affect your course grade.”

After the post-check, use this task for Clipcademy participants.

“Choose DNA replication, open the review, and reach a question. Choose the option we specify as incorrect so we can observe the follow-up interface. Watch that explanation, answer the next question, and return home.”

Record interventions as assistance. Do not explain buttons unless a participant cannot continue. Stop the timer when the task is finished or abandoned. Record abandoned steps and technical failures.

Ask open questions first.

1. What did you think this tool would do when you opened it?
2. Where did you hesitate or become confused?
3. What did the follow-up explanation change for you, if anything?
4. How did this compare with the material you actually used for your last exam?
5. When would you next choose to use it?
6. What would keep you from returning?

Then ask for clarity and usefulness ratings from 1 to 5, where 1 means very low and 5 means very high. Ratings and stated intent supplement observed behavior. Do not count them as actual retention.
