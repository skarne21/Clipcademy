What does UV light do to your DNA? Watch these two neighbors, a C and a T. UV light hits them. Now they're stuck together by a covalent bond, a strong chemical link. That bond bends the backbone into a bulge. Like a kink in a garden hose. The bulge stops genes from being copied normally. UV light bonds neighboring C's and T's together, which makes a bulge.
