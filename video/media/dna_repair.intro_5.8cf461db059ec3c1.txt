Quick recap: three repair jobs. During copying, DNA polymerase proofreads. It fixes its own mistake right away. After copying, mismatch repair proteins find wrong bases in the finished DNA. And UV damage gets cut out and replaced. Think of fixing typos as you go, then checking at the end.
