Where do new pieces of DNA get added? Meet DNA polymerase. It builds the new strand. A strand has two ends, 5-prime and 3-prime. Polymerase only works at one of them. Think of a train that only boards at the back. New pieces join the 3-prime end. Click. Click. Click. DNA polymerase adds new pieces only to the 3-prime end. So which way does it grow? Swipe.
